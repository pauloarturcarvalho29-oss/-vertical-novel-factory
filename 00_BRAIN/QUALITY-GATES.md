# Professional Quality Gates

An episode cannot reach READY until every gate passes.

## G1 Story

- script approved
- character motivations coherent
- chronology coherent
- dialogue consistent with canon

## G2 Identity

- face consistent
- body consistent
- hair consistent
- permanent features consistent
- wardrobe continuity verified

## G3 Voice

- same canonical voice
- no robotic artifacts
- performance matches scene
- pronunciation correct
- dialogue intelligible

## G4 Cinematography

- framing intentional
- lens/FOV coherent
- eyelines correct
- screen direction coherent
- 180-degree rule respected or intentionally crossed
- vertical safe area respected

## G5 Image

- maximum practical generation quality
- no severe anatomy defects
- no identity drift
- no temporal or generation artifacts
- lighting coherent

## G6 Motion

- natural movement
- stable identity
- stable environment
- no distracting temporal artifacts
- motion follows direction

## G7 Edit

- pacing supports narrative
- cuts are motivated
- continuity is coherent
- dialogue timing is correct
- transitions are restrained

## G8 Sound

- dialogue intelligible
- ambience coherent
- Foley/SFX synchronized
- music does not mask dialogue
- no clipping or destructive artifacts

## G9 Subtitles

- exact dialogue
- correct timing
- readable typography
- safe placement
- no unnecessary obstruction of faces

## G10 Technical

- 1080x1920 master
- 9:16
- correct frame rate
- correct audio sample rate
- no accidental black frames
- no corrupt frames
- final encoding verified

## G11 Rights

- model licenses verified
- asset provenance recorded
- fonts/music/SFX cleared
- third-party material documented

## Release rule

Any BLOCKER fails the episode.

MAJOR findings must be resolved or explicitly approved before release.
