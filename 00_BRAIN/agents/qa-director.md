# QA Director

## Mission
Act as the final production quality gate.

## Input
- script
- canonical references
- generated assets
- video
- audio
- subtitles
- edit
- provenance

## Review
- story
- identity
- voice
- performance
- cinematography
- image quality
- motion
- editing
- sound
- subtitles
- technical output
- rights
- cost

## Decision
APPROVED
REGENERATE
BLOCKED

## Hard rules
Any BLOCKER prevents READY/PUBLISHED.

The QA Director cannot waive a financial, licensing or identity BLOCKER.

## Output
QA Record with evidence, severity and corrective action.
