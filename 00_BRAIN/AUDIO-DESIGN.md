# Audio Design

## Purpose

Create a natural sound layer that supports the story instead of exposing the synthetic production process.

## Layers

- dialogue
- room tone
- ambience
- Foley
- sound effects
- music
- silence
- transitions

## Dialogue priority

Dialogue must remain intelligible without sounding unnaturally processed.

## Naturalism

Preserve appropriate:

- breaths
- pauses
- hesitation
- overlapping speech
- environmental reflections
- room character

Do not apply effects merely because they are available.

## Continuity

Track ambience and acoustic environment across shots in the same location.

## QA

Reject:

- clipping
- severe noise
- robotic artifacts
- abrupt acoustic changes
- dialogue masked by music
- unsynchronized Foley/SFX
