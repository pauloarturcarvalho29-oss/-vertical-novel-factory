# MASTER PRODUCTION RULES

This document consolidates the user's mandatory production requirements with the project's zero-cost constraint.

## 1. Priority

These rules are higher priority than individual agents, prompts and workflows.

No module may silently weaken them.

Priority order:

1. User's explicit mandatory rules
2. Zero-cost / no-debt rule
3. Production Bible
4. Identity and Voice Locks
5. Cinematography Bible
6. Workflow-specific settings
7. Model defaults

If a workflow conflicts with a higher-priority rule, the workflow must be rejected or redesigned.

## 2. Financial firewall

ABSOLUTE RULE: NEVER create a debt, subscription, recurring charge, trial that can convert to a charge, paid API call, paid cloud GPU usage, paid software license, paid asset, or other financial obligation without explicit approval.

The project defaults to:

- free
- open-source
- local
- self-hosted
- free-tier only when no charge can occur
- no credit-card-dependent services unless the user explicitly approves them

A tool with unclear pricing or possible automatic billing is treated as BLOCKED until its cost conditions are verified.

The uploaded framework's paid production budget, paid studio, paid actors, paid locations, paid post-production and paid software requirements are therefore NOT adopted as mandatory project dependencies.

## 3. Character identity is immutable

A character's canonical appearance must remain consistent across scenes.

Never silently change:

- face
- facial proportions
- eyes
- nose
- mouth
- jaw
- skin characteristics
- apparent age
- body proportions
- hair identity
- permanent marks
- tattoos
- scars
- permanent accessories

Expressions, pose, gaze, lighting and camera angle may change when the Shot Spec requires them.

Identity drift is a BLOCKER.

## 4. Voice identity is immutable

A character must have one canonical voice identity.

The project must never accept unexplained changes in:

- timbre
- vocal age
- accent
- language
- pitch characteristics
- pronunciation
- speaking style

The system must reject robotic, distorted or identity-inconsistent dialogue.

The preferred zero-cost route is real recorded human dialogue supplied by the production team or authorized performers.

Synthetic voice generation is NOT required by the project. If it is ever considered, it requires explicit user approval and must pass voice-quality and rights checks.

## 5. Visual authenticity

Master delivery:

- 1080x1920
- 9:16
- 24 fps target for cinematic delivery
- vertical composition designed from the beginning
- no blind horizontal-to-vertical crop

Every shot must specify:

- shot size
- camera position
- camera height
- lens/FOV target
- movement
- blocking
- eyeline
- screen direction
- composition
- focus target
- depth of field
- lighting
- duration
- transition relationship

## 6. Continuity

Track:

- face and body
- hair
- wardrobe
- makeup
- accessories
- props
- injuries
- emotional state
- location
- geography
- time
- weather
- lighting
- screen direction
- eyelines
- chronology
- dialogue facts

The 180-degree rule should be preserved unless an intentional crossing is explicitly specified.

## 7. Location authenticity

When a real location is used or represented, research must document:

- geography
- architecture
- street layout
- vehicles
- signage
- vegetation
- weather/climate
- time-of-day lighting
- local soundscape
- historical period when relevant

Do not insert anachronistic technology, clothing, vehicles or signage.

## 8. Cinematic restraint

Avoid visual artifacts that make the production look synthetic.

Do not use decorative:

- digital transitions
- fake lens effects
- excessive depth-of-field blur
- artificial HDR
- oversaturation
- unnecessary particles
- random camera movement
- morphing between shots

Natural cuts are preferred unless another transition has narrative justification.

## 9. Sound

Dialogue must remain intelligible.

Preserve natural:

- room tone
- ambience
- breathing
- pauses
- hesitation
- overlapping dialogue when appropriate
- Foley and environmental sound

Avoid excessive compression and processing.

## 10. Image/video quality

Generate and preserve the highest practical quality available within the zero-cost constraint.

The pipeline must distinguish:

- source generation resolution
- working resolution
- upscale/intermediate resolution
- final delivery resolution

Avoid unnecessary recompression.

## 11. Professional QA

Before READY, verify:

1. story
2. character identity
3. voice identity
4. acting/performance
5. cinematography
6. composition
7. lighting
8. color
9. motion
10. editing
11. sound
12. subtitles
13. technical encoding
14. rights/provenance
15. financial safety

Any BLOCKER stops release.

## 12. Uploaded framework rules intentionally not adopted as literal requirements

The following were excluded or adapted because they conflict with the project's zero-cost constraint, the existing AI architecture, or require verification before being treated as universal rules:

- mandatory professional actors paid for production
- mandatory professional voice studio
- mandatory paid physical locations/production
- mandatory paid professional post-production
- mandatory paid research/production budget
- mandatory paid software such as Adobe products or DaVinci Resolve Studio
- mandatory specific paid hardware/microphone/interface combinations
- mandatory insurance expenditure
- mandatory paid casting/audition process
- mandatory real-location filming when the project is using an AI production pipeline
- mandatory physical-film-production workflow when an equivalent free digital workflow is required

These exclusions do NOT weaken the quality targets. They change the implementation method.

## 13. Claims requiring verification

The uploaded framework states that an "AI Content" tag is legally mandatory on TikTok/YouTube in 2026. This statement must not be encoded as a universal legal rule until current platform policies and applicable law are verified.

The project should instead maintain a platform-disclosure checklist that can be updated when official rules are verified.

## 14. AI generation policy

The project's objective is AI-assisted vertical fiction production.

Therefore, the uploaded framework's blanket prohibition on all generative image/video AI is NOT adopted as a project rule because it conflicts with the established project objective.

However, AI generation remains subordinate to:

- character identity lock
- voice identity lock
- continuity
- cinematography
- quality gates
- rights/licensing
- zero-cost policy

The goal is not to produce visibly generic AI content. The goal is to use AI as a production instrument while preserving professional visual continuity.

## 15. No-debt guarantee

No workflow may:

- start a paid trial automatically
- require a credit card with uncertain billing
- consume paid credits
- deploy paid infrastructure
- use a paid API
- subscribe to a service
- create a cloud GPU bill
- incur any other charge

without explicit approval from the user first.

When in doubt, STOP and request approval rather than spending.
