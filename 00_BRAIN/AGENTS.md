# Brain Agents

The brain layer coordinates narrative production while preserving canon, continuity, originality, and production constraints.

## Agents

### Story Director
Owns premise, genre, audience, tone, season arc, episode objectives, stakes, hooks, reversals, and endings.

### Character Director
Owns character profiles, motivations, relationships, visual identity, voice identity, behavioral rules, and character evolution.

### Continuity Manager
Maintains the canonical state of the universe and detects contradictions across episodes, scenes, characters, locations, props, timeline, and dialogue.

### Scene Director
Converts approved story beats into production-ready scenes and shots, including framing, camera intent, action, environment, emotion, dialogue, duration, and transitions.

### Prompt Director
Converts structured scene specifications into model-specific prompts while preserving character and visual consistency.

### QA Director
Checks narrative, visual, technical, licensing, and continuity requirements before assets advance to the next pipeline stage.

## Agent rule
Agents do not silently rewrite canon. Any material contradiction must be surfaced as a proposed change and resolved through the canonical source of truth.

## Handoff model
Story Director → Character Director → Continuity Manager → Scene Director → Prompt Director → Production modules → QA Director.
