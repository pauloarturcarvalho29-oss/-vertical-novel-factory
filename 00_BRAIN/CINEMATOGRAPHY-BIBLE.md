# Cinematography Bible

## Purpose

Define a consistent camera language for the series.

## Required Shot Spec

Every shot must specify:

- shot_id
- scene_id
- shot size
- camera angle
- camera height
- lens/FOV target
- camera movement
- subject position
- blocking
- eyeline
- screen direction
- composition
- focus target
- depth of field
- lighting
- color intent
- duration
- transition in
- transition out

## Camera language

Camera choices must support story, character and emotion.

Examples:

- wide shots establish geography
- medium shots support interaction
- close-ups emphasize performance
- extreme close-ups emphasize specific details
- OTS shots establish relational perspective
- POV shots require clear narrative motivation

Avoid random camera movement.

## Continuity

The camera system must track:

- axis
- screen direction
- eyelines
- geography
- movement continuity
- focal relationship

Intentional violations must be explicitly declared in the Shot Spec.

## Vertical frame

All compositions are designed for 9:16 from the beginning.

Do not treat vertical as a crop of a horizontal master.

## Quality target

The final image should read as deliberate cinematography rather than a collection of disconnected AI generations.
