# Realism Standard

## Visual target

The uploaded reference is a STYLE AND REALISM REFERENCE, not a source for copying a real person's identity.

The production target is photorealistic live-action realism with believable Brazilian human characteristics.

## Human realism

Characters should have natural variation rather than synthetic perfection:

- realistic facial asymmetry
- natural skin texture
- visible pores when appropriate
- subtle freckles, marks and pigmentation
- natural under-eye variation
- realistic lips and teeth
- believable hair density and individual strands
- natural baby hairs and flyaways
- realistic eyebrows
- anatomically plausible hands
- natural body proportions
- subtle expression asymmetry
- realistic posture and micro-movements

Avoid:

- porcelain/plastic skin
- excessive facial symmetry
- beauty-filter appearance
- generic influencer-face construction
- exaggerated eyes
- impossible jawlines
- over-smoothed skin
- CGI appearance
- excessive sharpness
- artificial HDR
- generic "AI beauty" aesthetics

## Brazilian character design

Brazilian identity must be treated as culturally and phenotypically diverse.

Do not reduce "Brazilian" to one fixed appearance.

For each character, the Story/Character Director must define a specific background, family/heritage context when narratively relevant, region, age, lifestyle and physical characteristics.

The visual result should emerge from the character specification, not from stereotypes.

## Photography target

The reference style emphasizes:

- natural daylight
- practical environmental lighting
- believable exposure
- realistic shadows
- photographic depth
- natural lens rendering
- subtle sensor/grain characteristics
- realistic skin response to light
- environmental reflections
- authentic backgrounds
- documentary/live-action feeling

## Camera realism

Prefer plausible photographic choices:

- 24/35/50/85mm-equivalent perspectives when appropriate
- realistic depth of field
- physically plausible perspective
- natural camera height
- motivated framing
- restrained camera movement

Do not use shallow depth of field merely to hide generation defects.

## Character identity

Realism never overrides the Identity Lock.

The character must remain the same person while allowing natural expression, lighting, pose, wardrobe and camera changes.

## Approval benchmark

A candidate should be rejected if a viewer would reasonably perceive it as:

- plastic
- CGI
- overprocessed
- generic AI portrait
- anatomically implausible
- culturally stereotyped
- inconsistent with the canonical character

The target is: "this could plausibly be a frame from a professionally photographed live-action production."
