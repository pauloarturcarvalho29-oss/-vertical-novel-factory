# Show Bible

## Project
Vertical Novel Factory

## Canon
This document is the source of truth for the fictional universe of each production.

## Required sections
- Premise
- Genre
- Audience
- Tone
- World rules
- Main characters
- Supporting characters
- Relationships
- Character goals and conflicts
- Visual identity
- Locations
- Timeline
- Continuity rules
- Forbidden contradictions
- Episode arc

## Production rule
Any generated scene that contradicts established canon must be revised or rejected before entering the episode master.
