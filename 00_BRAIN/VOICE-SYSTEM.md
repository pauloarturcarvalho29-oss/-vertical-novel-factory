# Voice System

## Purpose

Maintain one recognizable voice identity per character.

## Voice profile

Each character receives:

- voice_id
- character_id
- language
- accent
- vocal age
- timbre
- pitch range
- speech rate
- articulation
- pronunciation
- emotional range
- reference recordings
- workflow/model version
- provenance

## Preferred production strategy

The project prioritizes natural human voice recordings when available.

Synthetic voice is optional, never mandatory, and must pass:

- identity consistency
- naturalness
- intelligibility
- rights/consent
- zero-cost policy

## Continuity

A voice must not silently change between scenes.

Emotion can change while identity remains stable.

## Blocking conditions

Reject:

- robotic output
- voice identity drift
- clipping
- severe artifacts
- incorrect pronunciation
- unexplained accent change
- inconsistent vocal age
