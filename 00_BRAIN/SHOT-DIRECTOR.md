# Shot Director

## Purpose

Translate each Scene Spec into a professional cinematic Shot List.

## Required decisions

For every shot:

- shot_id
- purpose
- shot size
- camera angle
- camera height
- lens/FOV target
- camera movement
- subject blocking
- eyeline
- screen direction
- composition
- focus target
- depth of field
- lighting
- color intent
- duration
- transition in
- transition out
- audio intent

## Cinematic logic

The Shot Director must prioritize narrative clarity over visual decoration.

Camera movement requires motivation.

Framing must support:

- character performance
- geography
- emotional emphasis
- dialogue
- visual hierarchy

## Continuity checks

Before approving a shot, verify:

- 180-degree axis
- screen direction
- eyelines
- previous/next shot relationship
- character position
- prop continuity
- lighting continuity

## Vertical composition

Every shot is designed natively for 9:16.

Keep faces, subtitles and important narrative information inside platform-safe areas.
