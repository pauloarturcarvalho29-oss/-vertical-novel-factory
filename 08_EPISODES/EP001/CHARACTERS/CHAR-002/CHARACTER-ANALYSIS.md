## Core identity

**Name:** Rafael Costa  
**Age:** 24  
**Role:** Student of Medicine  
**Narrative function:** Socially confident peer who can move naturally between humor, loyalty and impulsiveness.

## Human appearance target

Rafael must look like a real Brazilian young adult, not a beauty-model template.

### Face

- medium-brown skin with natural tonal variation
- oval-to-rectangular facial structure
- moderately broad forehead
- defined but natural cheek structure
- strong, slightly rounded jaw
- medium-wide nose with a natural bridge and base
- medium lips, with a slightly fuller lower lip
- dark brown eyes
- naturally asymmetric eyebrows
- subtle under-eye texture
- natural facial asymmetry
- realistic ears and hairline

### Hair

- short, dense, tightly curly dark hair
- irregular natural curl distribution
- realistic hairline
- individual curls and small flyaways
- no perfectly sculpted "AI haircut"

### Body

- young adult male
- natural athletic/slim build
- shoulders proportionate to torso
- realistic arms and hands
- no exaggerated muscularity
- normal posture variation

## Distinctive identity markers

These must remain stable:

1. compact tightly-curled dark hair

## Acting profile

Rafael should communicate through natural micro-expressions rather than exaggerated animation.

### Neutral
Relaxed face, attentive eyes, natural mouth posture.

### Genuine happiness
Eyes narrow slightly, cheeks rise naturally, smile is asymmetric and reaches the eyes.

### Amusement
Small closed-mouth smile, slight head tilt, brief eyebrow movement.

### Surprise
Eyebrows rise naturally, eyes widen moderately, jaw relaxes. Avoid cartoon-like widening.

### Concern
Brows draw slightly inward, lips relax, gaze becomes fixed and attentive.

### Fear
Subtle widened eyes, increased blinking, tightened jaw or lips, controlled breathing. Fear should remain physically plausible.

### Anger
Lowered brows, focused gaze, jaw tension, restrained mouth movement. Avoid theatrical grimacing.

### Sadness
Reduced facial movement, slightly lowered gaze, slower blinking, subtle mouth tension. Tears are optional and only when narratively justified.

### Relief
Exhalation, shoulders release, facial tension decreases, small natural smile.

### Embarrassment
Brief gaze avoidance, subtle smile suppression, small facial tension, natural posture shift.

His appearance should read as a specific individual from Brazil, not as an ethnicity preset.
