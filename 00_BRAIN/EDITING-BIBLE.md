# Editing Bible

## Objective

Build episodes with professional narrative rhythm while preserving continuity.

## Edit decisions

Track:

- shot order
- shot duration
- cut points
- reaction timing
- J-cuts
- L-cuts
- dialogue overlap
- music entry/exit
- SFX timing
- transitions
- subtitle timing

## Rules

Prefer motivated cuts.

Avoid decorative transitions unless narratively justified.

Do not use editing to conceal identity failures.

The edit should preserve:

- screen direction
- eyelines
- spatial geography
- emotional progression
- dialogue continuity

## Vertical delivery

Master:

- 1080x1920
- 9:16
- target 24 fps
- platform-safe subtitles and graphics

## Versioning

Every exported master receives a unique version ID.
